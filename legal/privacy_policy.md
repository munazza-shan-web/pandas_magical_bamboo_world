# Privacy Policy — Panda's Magical Bamboo World

**Effective date:** 21 November 2026

Panda's Magical Bamboo World is a casual interactive game. This starter release is designed to work locally on your device and does not require an account.

## Information we collect
The game does not intentionally collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. Game progress (adventure stars, bamboo, badges, Bao's care stats and settings) is saved only on your device using local storage and is never sent to a server. You can erase it any time from Settings > Reset progress or by clearing the app's data. This project does not include advertising, analytics, location tracking, or third-party account sign-in SDKs.

## Children
The game is designed to be family-friendly. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents or guardians can contact us with questions or concerns.

## Permissions
The starter project does not request access to contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, or photos. If future versions add features that need permissions or third-party services, this policy will be updated before those features are released.

## Data retention and security
Because this release does not operate a user account or transmit game data to our servers, we do not maintain a player-data database. Your device's own operating-system backup and security settings may apply.

## Changes
We may update this policy when the game changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.
